The houses

Biggest cuts first. "vs suburb median" compares today's asking price with the median sold price for that bedroom count in the suburb; green is 5% or more below it.

Monthly house values are Cotality's Home Value Index median house value for Perth at the end of each month (March, May, June, August and September 2026); the April and July reports could not be read. Cotality's monthly change comes from its index and can differ from the change between two median values. The 3 and 4 bedroom figures are REIWA's median house sale prices for the 12 months to September 2026, published 17 October 2026. Weekly figures are DropBee's weekly market reports for Western Australia, 20 April to 20 September 2026; DropBee had not published later weekly reports when this was refreshed on 23 October. DropBee widened its tracking during the period (the 11–17 May jump coincides with a national spike), so compare recent weeks with each other rather than with April and May, and the 31 Aug–6 Sep week looks short across the whole country. A drop compares a listing's current asking price with its first asking price, for homes still for sale; it is not a change in sold prices. Suburb figures cover all property types, from DropBee suburb pages refreshed 23 October 2026. The house section covers houses only; each DropBee suburb page shows its 25 most recent price moves, so busy suburbs such as Baldivis and Canning Vale are a recent sample. Median sold prices for 3 and 4 bedroom houses are from Domain suburb profiles (sales in the last 12 months), checked 17 October 2026; a median from few sales is less reliable. Suburb boundaries are WA localities published as GeoJSON by tonywr71 on GitHub; the Swan River sits inside suburb boundaries, so it isn't drawn. Grey suburbs on the map were not collected.
